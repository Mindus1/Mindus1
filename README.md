Just doing things out of boredom. Maybe you can use them.

Mostly small tools and mods for games I play.

**osu!**

- [osu-tablet-optimizer](https://github.com/Mindus1/osu-tablet-optimizer): reads your replays and suggests OpenTabletDriver area changes based on where your jumps land. Rust, runs on Windows, Linux and macOS.

**Lawgivers II** (BepInEx 6 IL2CPP)

- [lawgivers2-mod-manager](https://github.com/Mindus1/lawgivers2-mod-manager): switch mods on and off from inside the game.
- [lawgivers2-mod-library](https://github.com/Mindus1/lawgivers2-mod-library): menus, HUD buttons and game events that the other mods share.
- [lawgivers2-cheat-menu](https://github.com/Mindus1/lawgivers2-cheat-menu): unlimited action points, money and black money.

**Discord**

- [spotifyActivityToggle](https://github.com/Mindus1/spotifyActivityToggle): Equicord userplugin with a button to turn your Spotify activity on and off.

If something doesn't work, open an issue on the repo.
